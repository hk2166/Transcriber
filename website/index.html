FAQ

Does Confab need an internet connection?

Only twice, ever: to download the app, and to download the speech model on first use (~460 MB). After that, everything works fully offline. Your audio and transcripts never touch a server.

What do I need for AI summaries and chat?

Install Ollama (free) and pull a model like llama3.2. Without it, Confab still records, transcribes, searches, and exports — summaries and chat simply switch off.

How does it capture the other side of a call?

Through BlackHole, a free open-source virtual audio driver. Confab's settings walk you through the one-time setup. Mic-only capture works with nothing extra installed.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13.3 or newer. Apple Silicon (M1 and later) is recommended — transcription runs about 4× faster than real time there.

Where is my data stored?

In ~/Library/Application Support/Confab on your Mac: a SQLite database, WAV recordings, and a local search index. Delete it all anytime from Settings.
