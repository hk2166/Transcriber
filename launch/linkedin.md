# LinkedIn launch post

## Primary

Three minutes into the most important meeting of my quarter, my AI notetaker just… stopped.

No transcript. No summary. Just me, afterward, trying to reconstruct an hour of decisions from memory.

I was annoyed. Then I got curious — and a little uncomfortable.

Because that failure made me realize two things at once: I'd been trusting my most sensitive conversations to a tool that could quit on me without a word — and was quietly streaming every word I said to servers I'd never thought twice about.

I spend my days building with AI and voice. So the obvious question hit me: why am I renting this — and handing over all of this — when I could build it to run entirely on my own machine?

So I did. Meet Confab. 🎙️

→ Live transcription as you speak
→ AI summaries, action items, and decisions after every call
→ Semantic search across every meeting you've ever had
→ Chat with any meeting and get answers cited to the transcript

The part I'm proudest of: it's 100% on-device. No cloud. No bot joining your call. Nothing uploaded, ever. You can pull your Wi-Fi mid-meeting and it doesn't skip a beat.
