# Product Hunt draft

**Name:** Confab

**Tagline:** AI meeting notes that never leave your Mac

**Description (260 chars):**
Confab records, transcribes & summarizes meetings 100% on-device. Live
transcripts, AI summaries & action items, semantic search, chat-with-your-
meeting. No cloud, no bots in your calls, no subscription. Free for macOS.

**Topics:** Productivity · Meetings · Privacy · Artificial Intelligence · Mac

**First comment (maker):**

Hey PH 👋

I built Confab because every meeting assistant I tried had the same fine print:
your conversations go to their servers. Some literally join your calls as a
bot participant.

Confab takes the opposite bet — modern Macs are fast enough to run the whole
stack locally:

🎙️ Live transcription (~2 s latency, Whisper on-device)
🧠 Summaries, action items & auto-titles from a local LLM (Ollama)
🔎 Semantic search across every meeting you've recorded
💬 Ask a meeting questions, get cited answers
📄 Export to Markdown / PDF / Word / JSON
🔌 Unplug your Wi-Fi mid-meeting — nothing breaks

It's free. Speaker labels and Windows are next.

I'd love feedback — especially on what would make you trust (or not trust) a
"local-only" claim.
